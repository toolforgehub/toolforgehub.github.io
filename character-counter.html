Free character counter for text and posts

Enter or paste your text above to count characters instantly. The tool also shows words, sentences and characters without spaces, which makes it useful for social captions, short bios, application fields, titles, essays and writing drafts.

Characters with spaces vs. without spaces

Characters includes every character in the text, including spaces, punctuation and line breaks. No spaces removes whitespace such as spaces, tabs and new lines. Some websites count line breaks differently, so compare the result with the exact platform or form you are submitting to.

Example

The text “Hello world!” contains 12 characters including the space and exclamation mark, and 11 characters without whitespace. The word count is 2. Try adding a second line to see how line breaks affect the total.

Common uses

  • Check a social media caption or profile bio before publishing.
  • Fit a title, meta description or message into a character limit.
  • Check text length in application forms and online listings.
  • Compare a short version of a paragraph with the original.

Counting notes

This page counts the text string in the browser. Some special symbols and emoji can be represented by more than one underlying character unit, so a platform may show a slightly different count for certain emoji or combined symbols.

Privacy

The counter runs locally in your browser and does not intentionally upload the text you enter to our website. Do not paste confidential information on a public computer.
