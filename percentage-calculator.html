Free online percentage calculator

Use the three calculation buttons to solve common percentage problems: find what percent one value is of another, calculate a percentage of a number, or measure percentage change from an old value to a new value. Enter numbers in Value A and Value B, then choose the calculation you need.

1. What percent is A of B?

Formula: (A ÷ B) × 100. Example: if A is 25 and B is 200, then 25 is 12.5% of 200.

2. Find B percent of A

Formula: A × (B ÷ 100). Example: 15% of 80 is 12. Enter 80 as A and 15 as B, then choose the second button.

3. Calculate percentage change from A to B

Formula: ((B − A) ÷ A) × 100. Example: a price that rises from 80 to 100 has increased by 25%. A negative result means the value decreased.

Percentage change vs. percentage points

A change from 20% to 25% is an increase of 5 percentage points. Relative to the starting 20%, it is a 25% increase. These describe different things, so choose the one that matches your question.

Tips

  • Use the same units for both values.
  • For “A is what percent of B?”, B cannot be zero.
  • For percentage change, the starting value A cannot be zero.
  • Check whether your question asks for a percentage, a percentage point difference, or a percentage change.
