About Unix Timestamp Converter

Convert between Unix time and human-readable date values. Unix timestamps represent elapsed time from the Unix epoch and are commonly used in APIs, databases and application logs.

How to use Unix Timestamp Converter

Enter a Unix timestamp or choose a date/time value, then use the corresponding conversion control. Check whether the timestamp is expressed in seconds or milliseconds before converting.

Frequently asked questions

Are timestamps in seconds or milliseconds?

Unix timestamps are commonly shown in seconds or milliseconds. Check the input unit carefully because the two formats differ by a factor of 1,000.
