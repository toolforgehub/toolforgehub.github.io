About Line Counter

Count the lines in a block of text, including total lines, non-empty lines and the length of the longest line. This is useful for checking notes, scripts, lists and text files.

How to use Line Counter

Paste or type text into the input area and select Count. Review the total line count, non-empty line count and longest-line measurement.

Frequently asked questions

Are blank lines counted?

The tool reports total lines and non-empty lines separately so you can see how blank lines affect the overall count.
