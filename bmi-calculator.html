Calculate body mass index

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in metres squared. This calculator accepts metric or imperial measurements and displays the numerical result with the standard adult BMI category ranges.

BMI formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². For imperial inputs, the calculator converts pounds and inches to metric units before calculating the result.

Standard adult BMI categories

  • Below 18.5: underweight range
  • 18.5 to 24.9: healthy-weight range
  • 25.0 to 29.9: overweight range
  • 30.0 or above: obesity range

Limitations

BMI is a screening measure, not a direct measurement of body fat or a diagnosis. It does not distinguish muscle from fat, and interpretation can vary with age, sex, ethnicity, pregnancy and health context. Children and teenagers require age- and sex-specific charts rather than adult categories.

Health information notice

This page provides a mathematical estimate for general information only and does not provide medical advice. Discuss health concerns or an individual BMI result with a qualified healthcare professional.
