About CSV Viewer

The CSV Viewer displays comma-separated text as a table so you can quickly inspect its rows and columns. It is intended for quick viewing of pasted CSV data.

How to use CSV Viewer

Paste CSV content into the input area and select Render. Check the displayed table for the expected columns and rows; quoted values should be reviewed carefully when the source is complex.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a large CSV file?

The tool renders pasted text in your browser. Very large datasets may slow down the page or exceed your device's available memory.
