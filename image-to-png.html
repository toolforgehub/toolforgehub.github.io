About Image to PNG Converter

Convert a browser-supported image into a PNG file. PNG is a lossless image format that is useful for screenshots, graphics, logos and images that need transparency.

How to use Image to PNG Converter

Select an image from your device, wait for it to load, and choose Convert & Download. Open the downloaded PNG to confirm that the result looks as expected.

Frequently asked questions

Does PNG preserve transparency?

PNG supports transparency. The result depends on the transparency in the original image and how the browser decodes it.
