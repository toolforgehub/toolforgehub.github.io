Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how ToolForgeHub handles information when you use the free tools and pages at toolforgehub.github.io. ToolForgeHub is a static website. Individual tools may work entirely in your browser or connect to a third-party service when that function requires it.

Information you enter into tools

Text, images and PDF files selected for browser-based tools are generally processed on your device by the page's JavaScript and browser APIs. The site does not intentionally upload those selected files or text to a ToolForgeHub application server. Do not use a shared device for confidential information, and remember that your browser, extensions or device may have their own data-handling practices.

Third-party services and external requests

Some features rely on third-party code or services. For example, PDF and image conversion features may load libraries such as pdf-lib, PDF.js, JSZip or heic2any from public CDNs including jsDelivr or cdnjs. The QR code generator loads its library from jsDelivr. The currency converter requests public exchange-rate data from the Frankfurter API; the currency pair is sent in that request, while the entered amount is calculated in your browser. These providers may receive normal connection information such as your IP address, browser details and request metadata. Their own privacy terms govern their services.

ToolForgeHub does not control third-party providers and cannot guarantee their availability, security practices or retention periods. Review the provider's privacy information before using a feature that depends on an external service.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current website code does not require an account, collect payments or intentionally use a ToolForgeHub account cookie to operate the tools. This repository version contains advertising placement markers, not active Adsterra ad tags. If third-party advertising or analytics is added later, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies and may process device or usage information. This policy should be reviewed and updated when such services are enabled.

Hosting and technical information

The website is delivered through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider and network services may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser type, requested page and request time, as part of delivering and protecting the website. Refer to GitHub's published privacy terms for details about its processing.

Children's privacy

ToolForgeHub is a general-purpose utility website and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit personal or sensitive information through public feedback channels.

External links

Some pages link to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. Check their policies before sharing information with them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website adds, removes or changes features, hosting, advertising or third-party services. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or website concerns, use the ToolForgeHub GitHub issue tracker. Posts there are public; do not include passwords, private documents or sensitive personal information.

This page provides general website information and is not legal advice.

ToolForgeHub provides simple browser-based utilities for everyday tasks.
