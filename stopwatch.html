    Free stopwatch online

    Use this digital stopwatch to measure elapsed time in minutes, seconds and hundredths of a second. Start, pause, record laps and reset the timer without installing an app.

    How to use the stopwatch

    1. Choose Start to begin measuring.
    2. Choose Lap to record the current elapsed time without stopping.
    3. Choose Start again after pausing to resume.
    4. Choose Reset to clear the elapsed time and lap list.

    Accuracy notes

    The stopwatch uses the browser's high-resolution performance clock while the page is active. Background tabs and device sleep can affect visual updates, so use dedicated timing equipment for official competitions or safety-critical measurements.
