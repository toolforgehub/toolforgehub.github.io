Crop an image online

Use the image cropper to select a rectangular section by entering its pixel coordinates and dimensions. This works well when you know the exact crop size required for a profile picture, thumbnail, banner or product image.

How to crop an image

  1. Select an image from your device.
  2. Enter the left and top position of the crop area.
  3. Enter the output width and height in pixels.
  4. Choose Crop & Download to save the cropped PNG.

Understanding crop coordinates

The X coordinate measures pixels from the left edge of the original image. The Y coordinate measures pixels from the top edge. Width and height describe the crop rectangle. The crop area must fit completely inside the original image.

Privacy

The crop operation uses the browser canvas and does not intentionally upload the selected image to ToolForgeHub. Large images may use additional memory. Keep a copy of your original file before cropping.
