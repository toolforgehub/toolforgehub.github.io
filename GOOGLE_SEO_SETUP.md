# ToolForgeHub Google SEO setup

Website: https://toolforgehub.github.io/
Sitemap: https://toolforgehub.github.io/sitemap.xml
Robots: https://toolforgehub.github.io/robots.txt

After uploading the full upgrade package:
1. Confirm the home page loads.
2. Confirm sitemap.xml and robots.txt do not show 404.
3. In Google Search Console, add `https://toolforgehub.github.io/` as a URL-prefix property.
4. Submit `sitemap.xml`.
5. Inspect important category/tool URLs and request indexing.
6. Watch queries, impressions and clicks, then build related pages based on real demand.

Google does not guarantee indexing or a specific ranking position.
