Convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG

HEIC and HEIF are efficient image formats used by many Apple devices. Some websites, forms and older applications accept JPG more consistently. This converter changes selected HEIC or HEIF photos into JPEG images and packages them in a ZIP file.

How to convert HEIC to JPG

  1. Select one or more HEIC or HEIF files.
  2. Choose Convert to JPG.
  3. Wait while the browser converts each image.
  4. Download the ZIP and extract the JPG files.

Why convert HEIC to JPG?

JPG is widely supported by websites, email clients, online forms and image editors. HEIC can save storage space, but it is not accepted by every upload form. Keep the original HEIC files if you may need their original quality or metadata later.

Privacy and compatibility

Selected files are read in your browser. The conversion library is downloaded from a public CDN; the tool does not intentionally send your image files to ToolForgeHub. Conversion may fail for unsupported HEIC variants or very large images. The resulting JPG may not retain all HEIC metadata.
