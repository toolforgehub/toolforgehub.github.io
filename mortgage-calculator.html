Estimate your monthly mortgage payment

This mortgage calculator estimates principal and interest for a fixed-rate loan. You can also add annual property tax and home insurance estimates to see how those amounts change the monthly total.

What the calculator includes

  • Principal and interest: based on the loan amount, annual interest rate and selected repayment term.
  • Property tax: annual estimate divided into 12 monthly amounts.
  • Home insurance: annual estimate divided into 12 monthly amounts.

Mortgage payment formula

For a fixed-rate loan, the monthly principal-and-interest estimate uses the standard amortization formula: M = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments. A zero-interest loan is calculated as principal divided by the number of payments.

What is not included?

This estimate does not automatically include closing costs, lender fees, private mortgage insurance, HOA fees, utilities, maintenance or changes in taxes and insurance. Actual lender quotes and escrow amounts can differ.

Important

This is an educational calculator, not a loan offer or financial advice. Compare written estimates from lenders and review the full loan terms before making a decision.
