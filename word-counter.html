Free online word counter

Paste or type text in the box above to see the word count, character count, character count without spaces and sentence count update as you type. Use it when checking an essay, assignment, article, email, product description or social media post.

How this word counter works

Words are separated by spaces, tabs or line breaks. Punctuation stays attached to the word around it, so a word such as “hello!” is counted as one word. The character total includes spaces and line breaks; the “No spaces” total removes whitespace. Sentence counting looks for common sentence-ending punctuation such as a period, question mark or exclamation mark.

Quick example

For the text “Hello world!”, the tool counts 2 words, 12 characters including the space and punctuation, and 11 characters without whitespace. Results update immediately, so there is no separate submit button.

When to use a word counter

  • Check essay, report and assignment word limits.
  • Keep a blog post, product description or email within a target length.
  • Compare a draft before and after editing.
  • Check both words and characters before posting to a form or social platform.

Privacy

The counting logic runs in this page in your browser. The text you type is not sent to our server by the counter script. Avoid pasting sensitive information into any shared or public computer.

Word counter FAQs

How does this word counter count words?

It treats groups of text separated by spaces, tabs or line breaks as words. Punctuation remains attached to the surrounding word.

Does the character count include spaces?

Yes. The Characters total includes spaces and line breaks. The No spaces total removes all whitespace characters.

Can I use it for essays and social media posts?

Yes. Paste or type your draft to check its length before submitting an assignment, article, email, product description or social media post.

Does this tool save the text I type?

The counting script runs in your browser and does not send the text to the ToolForgeHub server. Avoid entering sensitive text on a shared device.
