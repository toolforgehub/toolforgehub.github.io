Compare two text versions

Paste two versions of a text to compare their whitespace-separated words by position. The checker reports where the tokens differ and is useful for reviewing drafts, copied text and simple content changes.

How to compare text

Paste the original text into Text A and the revised version into Text B, then select Compare. The page lists differing positions and shows the corresponding token from each version. It displays up to 30 differences.

Frequently asked questions

Does the comparison preserve formatting?

The current comparison focuses on whitespace-separated text tokens. It is not a character-by-character or rich document comparison tool.
