Read text aloud in your browser

This free text-to-speech tool turns typed text into spoken audio using the Web Speech API supported by many modern browsers. It is useful for listening to a short passage, checking how writing sounds, accessibility and language practice.

How to use text to speech

  1. Type or paste text into the text box.
  2. Choose a voice if your browser provides more than one.
  3. Adjust speed, pitch and volume.
  4. Select Speak, then pause, resume or stop playback.

Why are voice options different?

Voices are provided by the browser and operating system. A device may have only a small number of installed voices, and some languages may require additional system voices. The available voice list can differ between desktop and mobile browsers.

Privacy

This page uses the browser's speech synthesis interface. ToolForgeHub does not send the text to its own server. Some operating systems may use their own speech service depending on the selected voice and system settings.
