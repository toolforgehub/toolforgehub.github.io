Convert currencies online

Use this currency converter to calculate an estimated value between supported currencies. Choose the starting currency, destination currency and amount, then select Convert. The result includes the rate date returned by the rate provider.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

This tool requests public reference rates from the Frankfurter API. The service aggregates data from central banks and official sources; rates are updated as providers publish them. It does not require an API key. A network connection is required to load a rate.

Important: reference rates are not transaction quotes

Bank transfers, credit cards, cash exchanges and payment services may use different rates and add fees or spreads. Do not use this calculator as a guaranteed quote for a payment, remittance or financial trade. Check your bank or provider before sending money.

How to get a useful estimate

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  2. Choose the original and destination currencies.
  3. Select Convert.
  4. Check the rate date and compare the estimate with your provider's final quote.
