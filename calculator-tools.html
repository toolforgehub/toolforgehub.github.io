Everyday calculators in one place

Choose the calculator that matches your question. Each tool is designed for a specific calculation, with the inputs and result displayed on the page. Review your entries and units before relying on a result.

Available calculators

  • Percentage Calculator — find what percent one value is of another, calculate a percentage of a number or measure percentage change.
  • Discount Calculator — work out a discount amount and sale price.
  • Tip Calculator — calculate a tip and total bill.
  • Age Calculator — calculate completed years, months and days between dates.
  • Date Difference Calculator — find the time between two calendar dates.
  • Time Duration Calculator — calculate the duration between two times.
  • Average Calculator — calculate the mean, sum, minimum and maximum of entered values.

Percentage, discount and price calculations

Use Percentage Calculator for the underlying percentage formula. Use Discount Calculator when you know the original price and discount rate and need the final sale price. Use Tip Calculator when you need to add a gratuity to a bill. These calculations are related but answer different questions.

Date and time calculations

Use Age Calculator for a person's age between a birth date and an as-of date. Use Date Difference for the number of days between dates, and Time Duration for elapsed time between clock times. Check whether the tool includes the start or end date before using the result for deadlines.

Calculation accuracy

Results depend on the numbers and units you enter. These tools are intended for everyday estimates and learning, not as a substitute for professional accounting, legal, medical or financial advice.
