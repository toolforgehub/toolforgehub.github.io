Estimate fixed-rate loan payments

This loan calculator estimates the monthly payment, total interest and total amount repaid for a fixed-rate, fully amortizing loan. Enter the amount borrowed, annual interest rate and repayment term.

How to use the loan calculator

  1. Enter the amount you plan to borrow.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate as a percentage.
  3. Choose a term in months or years.
  4. Select Calculate to view the estimated monthly payment and total repayment.

Loan payment formula

The calculator uses the standard amortization formula for equal monthly payments. The estimate assumes a fixed rate and payments made monthly. A zero-interest loan is divided evenly across the repayment term.

What is not included?

The calculation does not include origination fees, late fees, insurance, variable interest changes, taxes or other lender charges. Actual offers may differ depending on lender terms and borrower circumstances.

Important

This calculator is for educational estimates only and is not a loan offer or financial advice. Review the annual percentage rate, fees, repayment schedule and full contract before accepting a loan.
